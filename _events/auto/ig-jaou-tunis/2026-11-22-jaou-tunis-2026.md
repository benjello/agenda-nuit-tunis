JAOU TUNIS 2026
HYPERCULTE
LIVE CONCERT

Formed in 2013, the Swiss duo Hyperculte, composed of Simone Aubert and Vincent Bertholet, brings together disco, pop, krautrock, punk and post-rock in a distinctive and energetic sound.

Driven by sharp rhythms, musical improvisation and politically engaged lyrics, their work moves between experimentation, poetry and a powerful reflection on the world around us.
